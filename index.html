jornbowrl rclone

This OAuth application lets its owner use the rclone command-line tool on their own computer to manage their Google Drive.

What it does

With the owner's authorization, rclone can list, read, download, upload, create, update, move, and delete Google Drive files. Commands run only when invoked by the owner or their configured local automation.

This is a personal integration, not a public file-sharing service or an official Google product.

Your data

This informational website does not process Google Drive files or OAuth tokens. rclone communicates with Google directly. See the privacy policy for details and revocation instructions.
